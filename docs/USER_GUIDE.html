Phyllis's contact clean-up

How to use DeDupe Pro on your MacBook Air. Everything happens on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing changes on your phone, in Outlook, or on the Synology.

The one rule: never double-click a contact file (names ending in .vcf or .csv), and never open one in the Contacts app. Doing that can copy thousands of contacts into your Mac. Only ever open contact files from inside DeDupe Pro, using the steps below.

Opening the app

  1. On your Desktop, open the folder DeDupe Pro for Phyllis.
  2. Double-click Open DeDupe Pro. It opens in your web browser. It isn't a website, and it works without the internet.
  3. If the writing is small, click A A at the top right. Click ◑ to switch between a light and a dark background. The app remembers both.

Your two contact lists are already in the same folder:

  • Outlook contacts (iBuyPower): your main list from the big computer.
  • iPhone contacts: everything that was on your phone.

To open a list in the app, drag the file from the folder onto one of the two dashed boxes. You can also click the box and choose the file.

Job 1: Look up a contact

Use this when you need a phone number or a note, for example if your phone is lost.

  1. Drag iPhone contacts onto the first box, Main contact file.
  2. Click 📖 Just Browse Contacts.
  3. Type part of a name, number, company, or a word from a note into the search box. You can also click a letter.
  4. Click a name to see the whole card, including all of its notes.

When you're done, close the browser window. Nothing was changed.

Job 2: Find contacts that are only on your iPhone

Your newer contacts are on the phone but not in Outlook. This job makes a list of just those, so Philip can add them to Outlook later.

Part A: compare the two lists

  1. Open the app. If anything is already loaded, click Start Over.
  2. Drag Outlook contacts (iBuyPower) onto the first box.
  3. Drag iPhone contacts onto the second box.
  4. Scroll down a little and tick the box "Two files: only look for duplicates between the files."
  5. Click 🔍 Find Duplicates and wait a few seconds.
  6. Click 💾 Export Clean Contacts at the bottom right.
  7. Find the card titled Only in "iPhone contacts…" and click its 📱 Outlook .csv button. A file starting with ONLY_IN_ goes into your Downloads folder.

Part B: tidy up that list

  1. Click Start Over.
  2. Open your Downloads folder in Finder and drag the ONLY_IN_… file onto the first box.
  3. Make sure the "two files" box is not ticked, then click 🔍 Find Duplicates.
  4. Go through the groups the same way as in Job 3 below. The list is short.
  5. Click 💾 Export Clean Contacts and then 📊 Download Outlook CSV.
  6. Drag the new file (its name contains CLEAN_FOR_OUTLOOK) from Downloads into the For Philip folder.
That's it for Job 2. Philip adds these to Outlook carefully on his next visit. Please don't import them yourself.

Job 3: Go through your duplicates

The app finds contacts that appear more than once in your Outlook list and shows them side by side. You decide what to merge. It never throws away a note, phone number, email, or address.

While you're doing Job 3, please stop deleting duplicates by hand in Outlook. The app is working from a copy of your list made on October 26. Changes made in Outlook from now on would be overwritten later. It's fine to keep adding new contacts as usual; just tell Philip roughly how many.

Getting started

  1. Open the app. If anything is already loaded, click Start Over.
  2. Drag Outlook contacts (iBuyPower) onto the first box, and leave the second box empty.
  3. Leave the setting on Balanced 60%. Click 🔍 Find Duplicates.
  4. At the top, change All statuses to Needs decision. Now you only see the groups waiting for you. The sure-thing groups are already marked Will merge, and you don't need to touch them.

For each group

  1. Click the group's name to open it. You see each copy side by side, and below them a purple box called Merged result. That box shows what you'll end up with.
  2. Choose one:
    If…Click
    They're the same person or business✔ Merge
    They're different people (husband and wife, two doctors at the same office, a nurse you filed under the doctor)✖ Skip. They stay separate, exactly as they are now.
    Most of the group is the same person, but one card isn't✖ Not a dupe on that one card, then ✔ Merge for the rest.
    You're not sureLeave it. Come back later, or ask Philip.
  3. To choose the name that's kept, click one of the names next to "Name to keep" in the purple box. The other spellings are saved in the notes, so nothing is lost.
  4. To fix a name or number by hand, click ✏ Edit, type the change, and click Save & Merge. Please don't delete text from the Notes box.
  5. Made a mistake? Click ↩ Undo at the top.
Tips:
  • Start with the 95–100% tab; those are the easy ones.
  • The lower percentages are often not duplicates: a household sharing a phone, different departments at CPMC, or companies with the same 800 number. ✖ Skip is usually right there.
  • A yellow or red label like "Different organizations" means "look carefully".

Stopping and coming back

  • You can stop at any time. Just close the browser window. If it asks "Leave page?", click Leave.
  • Next time, open the app, drag in the same Outlook file, keep the same settings, and click 🔍 Find Duplicates. It asks "pick up where you left off?" Click OK.
  • Don't click Start Over in the middle of Job 3. That clears your saved progress.
  • Always use the same browser. Don't use a private window, and don't clear the browser's history or website data. That's where your progress is kept.
  • Save a copy at the end of each sitting, in case the computer forgets: click 💾 Export Clean Contacts, then download the Outlook CSV and the Audit log. Drag both into For Philip.
  • If it ever doesn't offer to pick up where you left off, drag in the newest file in For Philip whose name contains CLEAN_FOR_OUTLOOK instead of the original Outlook file. Everything you merged is already done in it. Groups you skipped will show up again; just click ✖ Skip on them again.

When you're finished

  1. Click 💾 Export Clean Contacts. Check that the two boxes Note integrity and Field integrity both say OK.
  2. Download Clean contacts for Outlook (.csv), Audit log, and Duplicate list.
  3. Drag all three from Downloads into For Philip, and let Philip know.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
A message pops up when you drag a file inRead it, click OK, and take a photo of it for Philip. Nothing has been changed.
The Contacts app opened and asks to add contactsClick Cancel. A contact file was double-clicked by mistake.
A spreadsheet (Numbers or Excel) openedClose it and click Don't Save.
The app didn't offer to pick up where you left offMake sure you dragged in the same Outlook file and didn't change the settings. If it still doesn't offer, call Philip before starting again. Your saved copy in For Philip still has your work up to the last save.
The page is blank or looks brokenClose the browser and double-click Open DeDupe Pro again. If it's still broken, try right-click → Open With → Safari.
Anything elseStop and call Philip. Nothing in this app changes your real contacts, so nothing can break while you wait.

Setup notes for Philip

1. Put the app on her MacBook Air (5 minutes)

  1. On your Mac, copy the Desktop folder DeDupe Pro for Phyllis to the flash drive. It holds Open DeDupe Pro.html, this guide, and an empty For Philip folder.
  2. On her MacBook Air, drag that folder from the flash drive onto her Desktop.
  3. Copy the two exports from the flash drive into the folder. Rename them to end in .txt, so a double-click opens TextEdit instead of importing everything into Contacts or Numbers. When Finder asks, choose Use .txt. The app recognises the format from the contents, not the name.
    • Outlook contacts (iBuyPower) 2026-10-26.txt
    • iPhone contacts 2026-10-26.txt
  4. Double-click Open DeDupe Pro. If Safari blocks local files or shows a blank page, right-click → Open With → Firefox. Turn on large text (A A).
  5. Test drive with her, 2 minutes: drag in each file and check the counts. The iPhone file should show about 10,900 contacts; the Outlook file should be close to Outlook's own count. Then do Job 1 once together.
  6. Keep the flash-drive copies as the backup. Eject the drive.

2. Why this is safe

  • It's the same single file as the website, opened from disk. I checked that it runs from a file in Chrome; Safari and Firefox weren't tested, so use Firefox (her usual browser) if Safari misbehaves. It makes no network requests with contact data; it only loads fonts. Excel reading is the one feature that needs the internet, and her files are CSV and vCard, so it isn't used.
  • It only reads files she drags in and only writes to Downloads. It can't touch Outlook, the Synology, her iPhone, or Mac Contacts.
  • Saved progress lives only in that browser's local storage on her Mac. It contains her choices and any text typed into Edit, never a copy of the list.
  • If her Desktop & Documents sync to iCloud Drive, this folder goes into her own iCloud. That's acceptable; just don't put it in a shared Dropbox folder.

3. Decision you're making by handing her Job 3

Her merges are made against today's snapshot of the iBuyPower list. That means:

  • Hand-deletions in Outlook from now on conflict with them, which is why the guide asks her to stop.
  • When you eventually write the clean list back, re-export Outlook first. Load the new export with her clean CSV in two-file mode, with "between files only" ticked. The Only in "new export" file is everything she added since today. Add those too.
  • The write-back itself is unchanged: PST backup, CalDav Synchronizer paused on both the iBuyPower and the Dell, import into a new non-synced folder, a 5-contact round trip Outlook → Synology → iPhone, and a rehearsed restore. Only then do the switch-over.

4. If her Mac refuses a file

Bring it back to your Mac and ask Claude to run the converter on it. It prints only counts, never contact details:

node tools/convert-contacts.mjs "<path to file>"
